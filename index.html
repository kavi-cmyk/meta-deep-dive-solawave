Solawave isscalingone ad.

1,386 live ads. 57 pieces of copy. One offer. Nineteen of the twenty highest-impression ads in the account are the same Buy-One-Get-One-Free promotion, and the “biggest sale ever” carrying them has been live for 146 consecutive days. This is a teardown of what Solawave actually buys, what endures, and what the top three landing destinations are being asked to do.

01 · The verdict

Scaled, not diversified

Enormous production capacity pointed at a single idea.

Solawave is not under-investing in Meta. It ran 1,386 distinct ads in 90 days across video, static and carousel — output most DTC brands cannot match. The problem sits downstream of production: those 1,386 ads carry 57 pieces of copy, 38 headlines and one offer. The account is scaling reach, not learning.

02 · Overview

What Solawave is & how it plays

A red-light-therapy device brand selling a $399 hero product on a permanent two-for-one, through a bank of statics it refreshes less and less often.

01 — The product

The hero carries everything

The Wrinkle Retreat Pro LED face mask — 320 medical-grade LEDs, four wavelengths (amber 605nm, red 630nm, deep red 660nm, near-infrared 830nm), FDA-cleared, 3-minute sessions. It absorbs 76% of all ad traffic. Secondary lines — Radiant Renewal Wand, Neck & Chest Pro, Bye Acne — together take under 4%.

02 — The strategy

Volume over variety

1,212 new ads launched in 90 days against 1,971 the quarter before — a 38% drop in launch velocity while the live count held. The bank is large and ageing. Across 1,386 ads: 57 distinct copy blocks, 38 headlines, 35 destinations.

03 — What they push

Speed, clearance, two-for-one

Top USPs by deployment: 3-minute treatment (517 ads), visible results (343), red light therapy (336), Buy One Get One Free (276), FDA-cleared (265), 4 clinic-grade wavelengths (261), hands-free (240), 320 LEDs (219).

04 — The funnel

Straight to the product page

Almost everything runs ad → PDP → cart. There is no quiz and no advertorial at scale, and the one page built to justify the second unit — get.solawave.co/why-both-masks — carries six ads while 466 ads promise a free second mask.

Finding · opportunity

The most-liked copy isn't in the impression leaders

The two most-liked ads — 1,303 and 1,100 likes — and the ninth all run the same body copy: “Want smoother, younger-feeling skin without the cost of in-office laser treatments?”, a benefit list, the LightBoost Topicals cross-sell and a named dermatologist quote from Daniel Gould, MD, PhD. It is the only copy in either list that puts a doctor's name on the claim. None of the 25 current impression leaders in section 04 runs it.

Likes build up over an ad's lifetime and these two have been seen for 90 and 98 days, so part of the gap is age. But the account's delivery has moved almost entirely onto the short BOGO block while its best-engaged copy sits outside the top of the pool. It is the cheapest test in this report: put the doctor-quote body back on the current BOGO creatives and compare.

Evidence — AdSpy #1 (1,303 likes, 640 comments, seen 1 Jun – 30 Aug), #2 (1,100 likes, seen 1 Jun – 7 Sep), #9 (127 likes, seen 8 – 23 Sep). Doctor-quote copy on 0 of the 25 highest-impression active ads on 29 Sep.

Finding · watch

“Replace the appointment” wins likes without a discount

Four of the sixteen most-liked ads (#8, #10, #15, #16) carry message 02 from section 08 — “You shouldn't have to book 3 months out…” — with a 60-day guarantee and no BOGO anywhere in the ad. All four are video, all four were first seen in July–September, and #8 reached 144 likes in 13 days of observation. This copy has no ad in Meta's current top 25.

It is the only non-discount message in the account showing organic pull, and it fits the evergreen, guarantee-led rung proposed in the 90-day plan.

Evidence — #8 Instagram video 144 likes (27 Aug – 9 Sep) · #10 Facebook video 98 likes (27 Aug – 9 Sep) · #15 Instagram video 13 likes, landing on /why-both-masks · #16 Facebook video 11 likes.

Finding · watch

Two BOGO ads draw one comment for every two likes

The #1 ad has 640 comments against 1,303 likes and #6 has 88 against 167, about one comment per two likes. Across the other fourteen ads the ratio stays at or below one in five, and #2, with the same copy as #1, sits at 0.15. A thread that heavy on a discount ad often means questions, complaints or price objections rather than praise. The comments themselves weren't captured, so this is a flag to read those two threads, not a verdict on them.

06 · The endurance cohort

What they keep paying for

Impression rank shows what the account pushed that week — on 10 September the live top twenty was entirely the BOGO wave launched 8 September, all of it two days old. The more useful cut is endurance: the creatives Solawave has renewed longest. Both sorts land in the same place.

Finding · new

Video is 54% of output and 0% of the endurance cohort

Solawave produced 753 videos in 90 days against 627 statics. Yet every one of the ten longest-running ads is a static image, and the only video in the 10 September top twenty — the Wand spot at rank 2 — had launched two days earlier and has no track record. Either video is being cut before it can mature, or static is genuinely carrying this account and half the production budget is mispriced.

This is answerable from Solawave's own ad account in an afternoon: pull 90-day retention curves by format. If statics survive 3× longer, the production mix is the single largest efficiency lever in the business.

Evidence — Media mix across 1,386 live ads: video 753 (54.3%), image 627 (45.2%), carousel 6 (0.4%). Top ten by run length: 10 image, 0 video, 0 carousel.

Finding · critical

The most proven creative points at the least-used URL

Six of the ten longest-running ads — including both 143-day leaders and both creator ads — land on /products/wrinkle-retreat-pro-light-therapy-face-mask, the legacy product slug that takes just 46 ads and 3.3% of current traffic. The remaining four point at the ?openExternalBrowser=1 variant. Not one ad in the proven cohort points at the canonical FDA-cleared URL that now carries 738 ads and 53% of the account.

The brand's endurance winners and its current volume sit on opposite URLs. Any read of “what converts on the PDP” is comparing two different pages' histories.

Evidence — Legacy slug: ads 1–5 and 9 (143 d, 143 d, 133 d, 133 d, 105 d, 97 d). Browser-flag variant: ads 6–8 and 10. Canonical slug: zero of the top ten, 738 of 1,386 live ads.

Finding · opportunity

The one ad that reached a different audience ran 105 days, then was killed

Ad 5 is the outlier in every dimension. An #ad advertorial, 1,797 characters, opening “Go to your bathroom right now and flip over your ‘clean' moisturizer. Count the ingredients. We'll wait.” — a clean-beauty ingredient argument for women who already filter their water and read every label. It ran from a media partner page with 7.2 million likes, not Solawave's own. It is the only ad in the cohort tagged to The Health Optimizer and the only one carrying Media/Press.

It ran 105 days, peaked at rank 26 of 150, and went inactive on 8 August. Nothing replaced it. This was the account's one genuine expansion beyond the beauty-seeker cluster — a persona sitting at 22 tags out of 1,386 — and it is the obvious thing to rebuild, because Solawave already knows it holds for three and a half months.

Evidence — Ad m1715053516322806, 105 days, 4 creative duplicates, last seen 2026-08-08, status inactive. Host page industry “media/news company”, 7,242,545 likes.

Finding · watch

Creator ads are house copy with the pronouns swapped

Ads 3 and 4 are partner placements live 133 days — the third- and fourth-longest-running in the account. Their copy is the brand's own BOGO block with “our best-selling” changed to “their best-selling”. No creator voice, no personal result, no reason the audience should weigh it differently from the house ad running beside it.

Creator inventory that endures 133 days is worth more than a find-and-replace. The endorsement is the asset being paid for and it is the one thing the copy does not contain.

Evidence — Ads m868648829597072 and m972674411869940, both 133 days from 2026-04-26, 6 creative duplicates each, best ranks 19 and 28 of 74.

Finding · critical

“Our biggest sale ever” has run 146 days

The copy block opening 🚨 OUR BIGGEST SALE EVER — LIVE NOW 🚨 is the second-most-deployed message in the account and its longest-lived ad has been in market for 146 consecutive days. A sale that never ends is not a sale; it is a price. Every scarcity cue layered on top — “limited time”, “before it ends”, “this offer won't last” — is testable by any returning customer and fails.

The cost compounds: prospects who bounce learn that waiting is free, and the account loses its last lever for genuine seasonal peaks. Black Friday is eleven weeks out and Solawave has already spent the word “biggest”.

Evidence — Copy block 74d7bcc7: 217 ads, longest run 146 days. Near-identical variant 4d7c2049 adds 51 more. Promotion/discount theme on 522 of 1,386 ads. BOGO named as the USP on 276.

Finding · critical

Two contradictory offers are live in the same week

On 5 September the account launched 18 ads promising “30% OFF + FREE GIFT — sitewide” pointing at the Labor Day landing page. On 8 September it launched the BOGO wave that now occupies the entire top of the account. Both are still live. A shopper who sees the 30%-off ad, clicks, then retargets into a BOGO ad is being quoted two different prices for the same mask inside 72 hours.

Beyond the trust cost, this splits the conversion signal: two offers, two destinations, two sets of learning, neither reaching significance.

Evidence — 18 ads launched 2026-09-05 on get.solawave.co/labor-day-sale-2026-red-light-therapy. BOGO wave launched 2026-09-08 across PDP, homepage and collections. Both sets carry status active as of 2026-09-10.

Finding · watch

Discounting is the default mechanic, not a peak tactic

Offer-led headlines — “Buy One, Get One FREE ✨”, “Buy One, Get Another FREE”, “Two Masks. One Price. Ends Soon.”, “30% off sitewide” — account for 499 of 1,382 headlined ads (36%). The brand-led alternatives Solawave has already written and proven run at similar volume but never reach the top of the impression pool, which suggests budget rather than performance is choosing the winner.

Evidence — BOGO ✨ 355 (26%) · Buy One Get Another FREE 111 (8%) · Two Masks One Price 17 · 30% off sitewide 16. Benefit-led: 5-Star Results 299 (22%) · One Habit 245 (18%) · They Have 132 LEDs 135 (10%) · 60-Day Guarantee 46 (3%).

Finding · opportunity

The sharpest claim is the least-used

“They Have 132 LEDs. We Have 320.” is the only headline Solawave runs that makes a falsifiable, competitor-relative claim — and the hardest one for a rival to answer. It runs on 135 ads (10%) and one recent variant is trending up while sibling creatives trend flat or down. In a category where every brand claims “clinic-grade”, a specific number that beats a named competitor's spec is the cheapest trust available.

Evidence — 135 ads, longest run 40 days. Comparison theme on 162 ads (12%). Supporting spec claims already in rotation: 320 medical-grade LEDs (219), 4 clinic-grade wavelengths (261), FDA-cleared (265).

Finding · opportunity

726,000 customers, used in 9% of ads

Solawave's copy cites 726,000+ customers, a 4.7-star average and a 60-day money-back guarantee — the three assets a $399 considered purchase most needs. Testimonial ads are 5% of the account and social proof 9%, while feature-listing runs at 75%. Before-and-after — the money shot of this category — is on 13%. The proof exists; it is not being put in front of people at scale.

Evidence — Testimonial 72 (5.2%) · Social proof 126 (9.1%) · Before & after 186 (13.4%) · Media/press 81 (5.8%) · Features/benefits 1,044 (75.3%). Guarantee-led headline on 46 ads (3.3%).

LP 01 · critical

One product page, four URLs, four learning pools

1,054 ads — 76% of everything Solawave runs — land on the Wrinkle Retreat Pro product page, arriving through four different URL spellings: the canonical FDA-cleared slug (738), a short slug (142), an ?openExternalBrowser=1 variant (128) and a legacy slug (46). Two further parameterised variants add a handful more.

Meta treats these as distinct destinations for Advantage+ optimisation, and on-site A/B tooling treats them as distinct entry points. The most important page in the business is being tested four ways at once and none of the four reaches significance. Any PDP change — a hero swap, a new proof module, a price test — is measured against a quarter of the traffic it should be.

Evidence — 738 + 142 + 128 + 46 = 1,054 (76.0%) resolving to the same product. Longest continuous run differs by variant: 80 d, 24 d, 101 d, 146 d.

LP 02 · watch

A Black Friday page running in September

The /collections/black-friday-skincare-deals-red-light-therapy-solawave collection takes 92 ads (6.6%) and has been in continuous use for 41 days. It functions as the brand's permanent deals page — but it wears a Black Friday slug in September, and the ads pointing at it carry BOGO, “Prime Sale” and post-40 biology copy with nothing to do with Black Friday.

Two costs. The slug is visible in the link preview and the browser bar, so an off-season promotional URL undercuts the “limited time” framing of the ad that sent them. And Solawave is burning its actual Black Friday asset eleven weeks early: when the event arrives the page has no novelty, no fresh index signal, and a cohort who already saw its “biggest sale ever”.

Evidence — 92 live ads (95 in tagged scope), longest run 41 days, in continuous use since a 2025-12-30 HSA/FSA flight. Current headlines landing there: “Buy One, Get Another FREE”, “BUY ONE, GET ONE — Join 700k Happy Users”, “They Have 132 LEDs. We Have 320.”

LP 03 · critical

The Labor Day page launched early, ran one day, still serves the wrong offer

All 18 ads pointing at get.solawave.co/labor-day-sale-2026-red-light-therapy launched on 5 September 2026 — two days ahead of the holiday. Each records roughly one day of run time. Their ranks land between 33 and 127 in pools of 147 to 168, so this cohort sat in the bottom half of the account for its entire life while the BOGO wave launched three days later took every one of the top nineteen slots.

The page is orphaned: the account pivoted to BOGO on 8 September, but these ads still carry “🚨 30% OFF + FREE GIFT” and still show active. This is the offer conflict in section 07, and the fix is a five-minute pause rather than a strategy change.

Evidence — 18 ads, all start_date 2026-09-05, all days_running 1, all status active as of 2026-09-10. Best rank in cohort 33/168; worst 127/168. Labor Day 2026 fell on 7 September.

LP 04 · opportunity

The page built for the actual objection gets six ads

get.solawave.co/why-both-masks — an advertorial answering why a buyer should take the face mask and the neck & chest mask — is the only destination purpose-built to justify the second unit BOGO forces on every buyer. It runs on six ads. Meanwhile 466 ads promise a second mask and send people to a single-product PDP that has to solve that objection in its cart.

Evidence — /why-both-masks: 6 ads, 2 days longest run. Supporting creative already written and live at rank 17: “Your face has SPF, serum, and a whole routine. Your neck and chest? They get the same sun — and most routines stop at the jawline.”

11 · The plan

Ninety days, no extra production

Solawave already makes enough creative. Every play redirects existing capacity rather than asking for more, and each names the number it should move.

This week · critical

Kill the offer conflict

  • Pause all 18 Labor Day ads and redirect get.solawave.co/labor-day-sale-2026 to the live BOGO destination.
  • Audit for any other live flight quoting a percentage discount. The account should quote exactly one price at any moment.

Moves offer discipline 2 → 5. Watch blended CAC and return rate on orders sourced from the two conflicting flights.

This week · critical

Collapse the PDP to one URL

  • Pick the canonical FDA-cleared slug; 301 the short and legacy slugs to it.
  • Rebuild the ?openExternalBrowser=1 cohort on the canonical URL with the flag preserved as a parameter, not a separate destination.
  • Re-point all 1,054 ads. This is a bulk edit, not a rebuild.

Moves traffic architecture 3 → 7. PDP conversion-rate confidence intervals should tighten within two weeks at 4× the sample.

Weeks 2–6 · critical

Retire “biggest sale ever”, build a three-rung ladder

  • Evergreen rung: no discount. Lead with the 60-day guarantee — the headline already exists and runs on only 46 ads. Risk reversal replaces price reversal.
  • Value rung: HSA/FSA as a year-round pre-tax mechanic, not a December flight. Lowers effective price without touching list price.
  • Event rung: reserve BOGO and the word “biggest” for two windows a year. Black Friday is eleven weeks out and currently has nothing left to say.

Moves offer discipline 5 → 8 and margin per order. Watch contribution margin, not ROAS — ROAS will look worse before it looks right.

Weeks 2–6 · opportunity

12 · Method

Method & limits

Every figure here comes from ads Solawave ran publicly on Meta between 13 June and 10 September 2026, observed through the Meta Ad Library and enriched with Atria's creative tagging. The window covers 1,386 live ads against an all-time library of 12,016.

01 — What the data is

Delivery weight, not performance

Impression rank is Meta's relative ordering of an advertiser's own ads by delivery. It says which ads Solawave put weight behind, not what those ads earned. Creative tags — theme, persona, emotion, angle, USP — are model-assigned and multi-label, so shares within a dimension sum above 100%. Coverage varies by field: hook tagging is based on 556 ads, copy and landing-page tagging on all 1,386.

02 — How the cohort was ranked

Endurance, not engagement

Section 06 ranks ads by continuous observed run length among creatives active between 1 May and 10 Sep 2026, deduplicated to one row per distinct creative. Run length stands in for endurance because renewal is the advertiser's own verdict on a creative. Per-ad like, comment and share counts are not available through this source, so no engagement figure is quoted anywhere on this page. Ranking the same window by delivery weight instead returns the 8 September BOGO wave — a different list, the same conclusion.

03 — What the data is not

No spend, no revenue

No spend, revenue, ROAS, CAC or conversion figure here is observed — none of it is public. Claims about margin and offer economics are inference from observed creative behaviour and should be checked against Solawave's own ad-account data before budget moves. Landing-page commentary is drawn from the URLs, slugs and the ad copy pointing at them; the pages themselves were not crawled — solawave.co is blocked by this session's network egress proxy.

04 — Creative assets

Hotlinked, not hosted

Ad images and video files are loaded directly from cdn.tryatria.com rather than copied into this site. If the CDN is unreachable or a file has expired, the card shows a placeholder and keeps its links to the creative and its Meta Ad Library entry. 38 assets are referenced — 4 videos with posters, 30 statics.

05 — Engagement data

Likes are lifetime, not per day

Section 05 comes from an AdSpy search for “solawave” (ad text and advertiser), seen between 1 July and 30 September 2026 and sorted by total likes, captured from a logged-in AdSpy session. The search returned 16 ads, not 25. Likes, comments and shares are cumulative totals on the Facebook or Instagram post, so older ads have had longer to collect them. “Seen” dates are AdSpy's first and last observation, not Meta's launch and end dates. Media is loaded from content.adspy.com and may not display outside an AdSpy session.

06 — Typography

PP Mori substituted

This page is built to the reference deep-dive's design system. PP Mori is a commercial Pangram Pangram face that is not on Google Fonts and could not be fetched here, so Archivo stands in at the same weights. “PP Mori” remains first in every font stack — self-host the two .otf files and the page picks them up with no other change. Space Mono is the reference face exactly.
